The transcript's last section prints how many of the five event kinds fired. Both
early runs printed:

    Kinds fired: move, capture, win, lose (4 of 5)
    Not observed in this run: crown. It is not claimed as working.

The cause was in the harness, not in the game. It put a RED man on (3, 6) and
moved it to (2, 7) expecting a promotion - but (3, 6) was occupied by a BLUE
piece, so the move was BLUE's, and BLUE promotes on the row at the OTHER end of
the board (y == 0, not y == 7). The move played. The move sound played. Nothing
was thrown and nothing looked wrong on screen. Only the "kinds fired" count
noticed that one of the five was missing.

The fix was one cell: (3, 6, 1) instead of (3, 6, 2). The final run reports five
of five, and the 14-line transcript in feedback_lines.txt is the fixed one.

This is recorded rather than deleted because it is the only reason to trust the
count: a harness that only checked "some audio played" would have passed all three
runs.
