# Task Cards - Participant's Copy

Print at A5, one card per page, or show the card on a second screen. Large type on purpose: the
card is read at arm's length while the phone is in the participant's other hand.

Each card carries **one** task. Never show the next card early - the participant should meet each
task without time to plan.

---

# Put your name on the player profile and save it.

Use a made-up name, not your real one.

## Card R - the debrief ratings

# How much do you agree?

Circle one number for each line.

|  | 1 = strongly disagree | 5 = strongly agree |
|---|---|---|
| I could tell what to do without being told. | 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 | |
| The pieces and the board were easy to see. | 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 | |
| The game told me when something happened. | 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 | |
| I understood why the match ended. | 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 | |
| I would play this again. | 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 | |

## Notes on the wording

- **"Catch"**, not "capture". The participant is a first-time player, and the script is written in
  the words a player would use, not the words the code uses. The task is identical either way.
- **"Crown ... into a Dama"** keeps the game's own term on purpose. If the word `Dama` means
  nothing to them, that is worth finding out at T4 rather than in the debrief.
- **"Finish the match"** does not say *win*. The question behind T5 is whether the end condition is
  legible, and a player who loses is just as able to answer it.
- **No card tells the participant which button to press**, how long a task should take, or that
  anything is being timed. A card that coaches makes the session measure the card.
