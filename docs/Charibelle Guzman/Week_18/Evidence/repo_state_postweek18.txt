The project listed four AI packages, two of which are the same plugin under two names:

  com.adjoint.editor        1.8.2             https://github.com/adjointdev/adjoint-beta-dist.git
  com.coplaydev.unity-mcp   10.3.1-beta.11    https://github.com/CoplayDev/unity-mcp.git?path=/MCPForUnity
  com.ramenvr.aura-unity    1.8.0             https://github.com/ramenvr/AuraForUnityPlugin.git

installed at once. The Console carried one compile error and eight GUID-conflict warnings:

  ...seven more, for Editor/Windows, Editor/Windows/Components, Common.uss, package.json, README.md and Runtime

`com.adjoint.editor` was removed from `Packages/manifest.json`; nobody in `Assets/` referenced either
