1. Before you start — there is no installer

This submission contains no APK. No Android build was produced for this project, so there is nothing to install on a phone. That is recorded as BLOCKED, with the reasons, in Week_18/Evidence/final_pre_submission_checklist.md §3.

To play the submitted game:

  1. Open the project in Unity 6000.6.0f1 (or newer 6000.x).
  2. Open Assets/Scenes/DamaGame.unity.
  3. Press Play. The game runs at the Editor's game view resolution.

Everything in the rest of this guide describes that build.

2.1 CHOOSE MODE

PLAY opens one screen that decides the match before it starts:

Control What it does
PLAYER vs AI you against the computer, at the level armed below
PLAYER vs PLAYER two people passing one device between them
EASY / NORMAL / HARD the AI level; the armed one is drawn in the brighter fill and named above the row
PLAYING AS: RED / BLUE which side you take; the choice is stored in the profile
BACK returns to the title screen without starting anything

Your side also decides which end of the board is nearest you: play RED and the red men start at the bottom, play BLUE and the whole board is turned half a turn so the blue men are at the bottom. A tap is read from the turned board, so the square you touch is the square that is played. The view does not turn again during a match, so two people passing one device keep one board.

3. The rules, as the game enforces them

Dama is played on an 8x8 board, twelve men a side. Men move one square diagonally forward; the board is drawn from above so both players can read it across a table.

  • A capture must be taken. If a jump is available to you, the game will not offer you a quiet move. Tap a man with no legal move and nothing will be selected — that is the rule, not a broken tap.
  • A capture chains. If your man lands where another jump is available, it keeps going until the chain ends. You do not choose to stop early.
  • Reaching the far row crowns a Dama. A Dama moves any distance along any diagonal, forward or back.
  • The match ends when a side has no men left, or has men but no legal move.
  • A match can also end level. Two counts end it in a draw: the same position, with the same side to move, appearing for the third time, or 50 turns in a row with neither a capture nor a promotion. A capture or a promotion resets both counts.

How to move: tap the man you want, then tap the empty square you want it to go to. The HUD at the top tells you whose turn it is and how many men each side has.

4. In a match

On screen Meaning
Turn: RED / Turn: BLUE whose move it is, in a two-player match
Turn: RED (you) / Turn: BLUE (AI) [thinking] against the AI: which colour is yours, and which one is the machine
PLAYER vs PLAYER / PLAYER vs AI - HARD the mode this match is being played in, under the turn line
Red: 12 Blue: 12 men remaining, both sides
PAUSE the pause panel (below)
HINT paints a legal move for the side to move in green. It suggests; it never moves anything, and the next tap clears it

Pause panel. RESUME returns to the match; RESTART starts a new board; UNDO takes your last turn back; SOUND turns the move and end-of-match audio on or off; MENU abandons the match and returns to the title screen. That last one exists because of a defect found in Week 15: before it, once a match started there was no way back to the title without closing the app.

UNDO retracts one turn — one move of yours, including a whole forced jump chain, because a chain is one turn and not three. There is no limit: press it again and again to walk further back. Against the AI one press also takes back the reply that followed, so the board always returns to you rather than to the machine. The draw counters come back with the position, so undoing a repetition does not leave the match one appearance closer to a draw.

End screen. The winner is named, the reason is written out (BLUE has no pieces left, BLUE has no legal move) and the counts are given. A level match says DRAW and gives the count that ended it — DRAW. RED 3-3, 50 plies without a capture. or DRAW. RED 3-3, the same position 3 times. RESTART plays again; MENU returns to the title.

The match is recorded in your profile when it ends — once, even if the end screen is reached twice. A draw is recorded as neither a win nor a loss.

5. Your profile

The profile stores everything on this device and nothing anywhere else. There is no account and no network call.

Field What it is
NAME up to 16 characters; type it, then SAVE
SIDE which colour you play, and which end of the board you sit at; set here or on CHOOSE MODE (section 2.1); wins and losses are counted against it
Matches / Wins / Losses / Draws your record; a draw is counted on its own line, not as a win or a loss
Current streak / Best streak consecutive wins
Fastest victories your three quickest wins, with the match number
RESET PROFILE erases name, record and best times — two presses, within four seconds

The two-press reset is deliberate. One tap that wipes an evening's record is a defect waiting to happen on a phone, and a confirmation dialog is dismissed by habit; so the button arms, changes its label to PRESS AGAIN, says what will be lost, and waits.

6. If something looks wrong

What you see Why What to do
Tapping a man selects nothing capture is compulsory and that man has no legal move tap a man that can jump
The game seems frozen after a match the end screen is modal, and the clock is stopped under it RESTART or MENU
SOUND does nothing in the Editor the audio device is the Editor's, and the clips are short it is a settings toggle, not a test
UNDO does nothing on the first turn there is no completed turn to take back yet play a turn, then pause again
UNDO in an AI match takes back two turns the AI had already answered, so its reply came back with your move that is intended — the board comes back to you
No vibration Handheld.Vibrate() is a no-op in the Editor unproven on a phone, and not claimed
The profile is empty after moving the project the save lives under Application.persistentDataPath, outside the project nothing to recover; a fresh profile starts
The profile says a save was rejected the file's signature did not match the old file is kept aside and a clean profile starts

7. Accessibility

  • Every control clears a 96 canvas-pixel touch floor at the project's 1280x720 reference scaler (48 dp at xxhdpi is 144 physical px; 144 / 1.50 = 96 canvas px). The two controls added in the post-submission revision also clear it: UNDO is 272x96 and HINT is 160x96.
  • Captions are 22 px bold and body labels 19–20 px, measured by the audit, not chosen by eye. UNDO's label measures 6.24:1 on its own fill and HINT's 12.44:1, both over the 4.5:1 text floor.
  • 25 control fills are measurable, and 17 of them sit within a fifth of the 3:1 non-text line — the closest is 3.03:1. All pass; the tightest ones have under 0.6 of headroom, which is the honest reading of that number.
  • What is missing: text does not follow the phone's font-size setting, and there is no screen-reader path. A player who needs either cannot use this game yet; that is stated here rather than hidden.

8. What the game does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet, and it has no accounts, ads or purchases.
  • It does not send anything off the device; the only file it writes is the profile.
  • It does not run on a phone in this submission — no build exists. §1 is the whole of that story.
